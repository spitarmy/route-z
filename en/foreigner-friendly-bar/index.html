Looking for genuine, foreigner friendly bars in Gion, Kyoto where you can relax without feeling intimidated or worried about language barriers? Welcome to Bar Route Z, a cozy, atmospheric hidden gem located on the 4th floor of Maruyama Building, just a stone's throw from Yasaka Shrine.

Why International Travelers Love Bar Route Z

🗣️ 1. English Speaking Staff & English Menus

No language barrier! Our bartenders speak English and our menu is fully translated with exact prices. Whether you want a recommendation on rare Japanese whiskies or a fun local conversation about Kyoto, you are always at home here.

💴 2. 100% Transparent Pricing — No Hidden Fees

In Gion, many travelers are cautious about mysterious cover charges. At Route Z, everything is clear: Table Charge is a flat ¥1,500 per person (tax included). There is NO 10-20% service fee and NO late-night surcharge. You pay only for your table charge and the drinks you enjoy.

🎤 3. All-You-Can-Drink & Karaoke Plan (¥5,000 Flat)

Want a complete night out without counting drinks? Enjoy our signature 2-hour All-You-Can-Drink & Unlimited Karaoke for ¥5,000 (Table charge and tax INCLUDED!). Draft beer, Japanese highballs, gin, cocktails, and karaoke included. The best deal in Gion!

🥃 4. Authentic Japanese Whisky Collection

Savor Japan's legendary single malts: Yamazaki (¥2,000), Hakushu (¥2,000), Hibiki, and Nikka Yoichi (¥1,200). Hand-carved ice, premium glassware, and authentic Japanese bar hospitality.

💳 5. Cashless & International Cards Accepted

We accept all major international credit cards: VISA, Mastercard, American Express, JCB, Diners, Discover, Apple Pay, and Japanese Transportation IC cards (Suica/ICOCA).

🚶 6. Walk-Ins & Solo Travelers Warmly Welcomed

No reservation needed! Traveling solo through Japan? Our counter seats offer a friendly, safe, and comfortable atmosphere to meet fellow travelers and local Kyoto regulars.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q. What time do you open and close?

We are open every evening from 20:00 (8:00 PM) until 3:00 AM (often later on busy nights). Drop in anytime after dinner in Gion or Pontocho!

Q. Where is Bar Route Z located?

We are located at Maruyama Building 4F, 422 Rinshacho, Higashiyama-ku, Kyoto. Just 1-2 minutes on foot from the main gate of Yasaka Shrine. Take the elevator straight to the 4th floor!

Q. Can I sing karaoke in English?

Yes! Our modern karaoke machine features an extensive library of English, American, and international pop, rock, and classic songs.
